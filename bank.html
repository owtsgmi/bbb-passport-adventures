🔔 Payout Alerts

Free browser notifications. No SL script, bot, group, or automatic payment.

The simple version

Passport Adventures can notify you through your normal browser when the recipient's unpaid reward balance reaches another 1,000 L$.

Nothing runs in Second Life. You still make the payment manually. The alert is only a reminder.

1. Enable alerts once on each device

1
Return to Adventures.
2
Under the reward balance, press Enable payout alerts.
3
When your browser asks, choose Allow.

Each browser/device subscribes separately, so you can enable your desktop, laptop, or phone independently.

2. Where the alert appears

Payout alerts show up as normal desktop/system notifications, not as a message inside the Passport Adventures browser tab.

Windows: look for the lower-right toast or Notification Center. Linux: look in your desktop notification area.

🔧 Admins: diagnose browser popups

3. Normal use

The server checks the shared reward balance automatically. When the number of available 1,000 L$ payments increases, subscribed devices receive a payout notification even if the Passport Adventures page is closed.

After you manually pay 1,000 L$ in Second Life, press Mark 1,000 L$ paid. Any remainder stays toward the next threshold.

Optional one-click Pay link

An admin can set up the recipient's Second Life Pay shortcut from the diagnostics page. Regular users do not need to configure anything here.
